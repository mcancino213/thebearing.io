About Nour El Nil

Founded in 2005, Nour El Nil pioneered the revival of the traditional Egyptian dahabiya — a wooden sailing vessel that once carried royalty and explorers along the Nile. Where modern cruise ships carry hundreds, each Nour El Nil boat carries eight to twelve guests in rooms that feel more like small suites than cabins.

The itinerary runs from Esna to Aswan over five nights, threading through villages and archaeological sites that appear on no mainstream tour. The Edfu Temple at dawn, with no one else present, is the kind of experience guests describe years later.

The chef prepares entirely from local markets each morning. Meals happen on deck as the Nile slides past.
