The Bearing Edit

The only lodge with an unobstructed view over the entire crater floor.

"The Ngorongoro Crater is the largest intact volcanic caldera on earth. &Beyond built a lodge where every room looks directly into it."
Twenty-five suites perched on the crater rim at 7,500ft, each with floor-to-ceiling glass looking south into the Ngorongoro Crater — a 260km² natural enclosure containing over 25,000 animals. Descend each morning into what Ernest Hemingway called the closest thing to Eden.
