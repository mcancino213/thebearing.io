Overview
Sixteen suites on a purpose-built vessel designed for deep-Amazon exploration in maximum comfort. Launched in 2021 after three years of construction, Aqua Nera operates out of Iquitos — the largest city in the world inaccessible by road — and penetrates tributaries no larger vessel can reach.
