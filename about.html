Our story

We built the site we couldn't find.

The problem with travel booking today isn't a lack of options. It's too many. And almost all of them optimised for the same thing — volume, not experience.

The Bearing started with a single observation: the hotels and cruises that genuinely stop you in your tracks — the ones you tell people about for years afterwards — are almost never the ones that appear first in a search engine. They're too small, too opinionated, too hard to describe in a star rating.

We built The Bearing to fix that. A platform that is, at its heart, a point of view. Fewer than 250 properties. Every one visited in person. Every one coming with an experience we've arranged ourselves — not a voucher, not a discount. Something you couldn't have organised alone.

And because we believe you shouldn't pay a premium for better discovery, we charge you nothing extra. We earn a commission from the property, the same way every travel agent always has. You pay exactly what you'd pay booking direct.

"The best hotel I ever stayed in didn't have a website. The owner's son answered the phone. I found it through a journalist who'd written one paragraph about it in 2009. That's the kind of place The Bearing exists to surface."

— Founder, The Bearing
