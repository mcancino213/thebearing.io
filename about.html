About The Bearing

We are not a booking site. We are an opinion.

The Bearing was started by one person who kept being asked the same question: "Do you know anywhere extraordinary I should go?"

The question always came from people who had already been everywhere obvious — who'd done the Maldives and the safari and the Amalfi Coast — and were now looking for the next thing. Not more expensive. More real.

The Bearing is the answer to that question, built into a platform. Thirty-four properties, chosen because someone on our team would personally go back. A membership that puts you in direct contact with the people running them.

We're headquartered between London and Cairo, which probably tells you something about our taste.
