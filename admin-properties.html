Use Resend (free · resend.com). Get a free API key, verify your sending domain, and emails go from your own address with full open/click tracking.
Reply tracking
Resend supports webhooks for open and click events. Point your webhook at a small backend endpoint (a single Vercel serverless function works) and it will automatically update the status on each card. Until then, you can manually log events by clicking a card's Replies button.
